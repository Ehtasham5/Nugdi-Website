About Us

NUGDi is a technology company that connects people with the best in their cities or globally in an advanced rating-driven system and hierarchical structure. We do this through our comprehensive B2B and B2C business model that serves all players and stakeholders in the food, beverage and grocery industry. The company aims to inspire a shift in the on-demand food and grocery industry.

NUGDi Service

NUGDi is a powerful all-in-one business management and appointment booking platform built specifically for salons, spas, clinics, wellness centers, and service professionals. It empowers businesses to streamline daily operations, manage appointments seamlessly, accept secure payments, track client activity, and drive revenue growth—both online and at the point of service.
